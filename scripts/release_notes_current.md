## 0.1.0-dev.31

- Multiplex non-default AWS Chime RTC data-message topics (such as `realtime.profile.v1` and interaction topics) over `chat` via `__chimeTopicEnvelope` in both the Web/macOS JS Chime driver and `flutter_aws_chime`, fixing immediate `RealtimeApiFailed` (status 17) disconnects on macOS after joining an AWS meeting.
- Resolve public vendor IDs (such as `aws`) to their concrete media engine (`chime` / `ivs`) in `RealtimeSdk.preJoin` when joining by `roomCode`, and include `vendor` and `engine` in `demo-server` `/rooms/discover` summaries.
- Prevent iOS `UiKitView` video tiles from rendering over Flutter modal dialogs: enable `clipsToBounds` on Chime's `DefaultVideoRenderView`, skip `BackdropFilter` blur on iOS modals in `RealtimeGlassDialog` and `showRealtimeGlassBottomSheet`, and keep `MediaTrackView` offstage via `MediaPlatformViewOcclusion` during `RealtimeMeetingPage` / `RealtimeLivePage` dialogs without unbinding active native tiles.
- Verification status: macOS Chime join-and-stay-connected E2E, `demo-server` checks, JS bridge tests, SDK architecture/quality checks, Chrome Web regressions, and single-SDK `pub publish --dry-run` pass; live iOS/Android physical-device verification of active video + dialog layering remains pending.
